Privacy Policy — Squish Sort

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This page is maintained by the app owner (“we”, “us”) to explain how Squish Sort (“the App”) handles information. The App is designed to be enjoyed without creating an account and without collecting personal information on our own servers.

Information stored on your device

The App saves your Fluff balance, owned cosmetic skins and jar themes, level progress, and your Daily Challenge streak in local device storage so you can pick up where you left off. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the App removes it.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not require sign-in.
  • We do not collect your name, email, or contact information.
  • We do not access your location (GPS), camera, microphone, photos, or contacts.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Usage analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played — which levels get completed, how the Daily Challenge and streak are used, and which languages and settings are used. These reports are anonymous and aggregated. They include an app-instance identifier generated by Firebase, your device model, operating-system version, and an approximate country derived from your IP address. We do not collect your name, email, or precise location, we do not set a user ID, and we do not link this data to your identity. We also receive aggregated ad-revenue reporting through the link between Google AdMob and Firebase. See Google’s Firebase privacy and security information.

Advertising

The App displays banner, interstitial and rewarded-video advertisements to support development, served through Google AdMob. Rewarded ads (“extra jar”, “undo”) are always optional — every level can be finished without watching one. When advertising is enabled in the store builds, the ads SDK may collect information such as your device advertising identifier, coarse location derived from IP, app interaction and advertising data, and technical device information in order to serve and measure ads. On iOS you will be asked for App Tracking Transparency permission before any tracking identifier is used; declining still lets you play, you may simply see less-relevant ads. See Google’s advertising privacy notice at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where required, the App uses Google’s consent mechanism (the UMP SDK) to ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

Your US state privacy rights

We do not sell your personal information for money. Using your advertising identifier for personalized ads may be considered “sharing”/a “sale” under some US state laws. You can opt out by declining the tracking prompt (iOS), withholding advertising consent, or limiting ad personalization in your device settings.

Children

The App is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can change your ad tracking choice at any time (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). You can limit ad personalization from your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising; Android: Settings → Google → Ads). Deleting the App removes the settings and progress stored on your device.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@solevia.app
