Privacy Policy — Bubble Pop Calm

Last updated: July 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sole Via Entertainment LLC (“Solevia”, “we”, “us”) handles information in connection with the mobile game Bubble Pop Calm (“the App”). The App is a simple relaxation game: you pop bubbles across five phases and your best scores and times are saved on your device. You can play without creating an account, and we do not operate servers that collect your personal information.

The App is supported by advertising provided through Google AdMob. The most important thing to understand is that we do not collect personal information ourselves, but our advertising partner (Google) does collect certain device and usage information in order to show and measure ads, as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following locally on your device, using your device’s local storage. It stays on your device, we cannot read it, and it is not sent to us:

  • Your best score and best time for each phase, and your most recent results.
  • Your settings, such as whether the pop sound is on.

You can erase this at any time from the Records or Settings screen (“Reset records”) or by uninstalling the App.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not require an account or sign-in.
  • We do not ask for or collect your name, email, phone number, or contact details.
  • We do not access your location (GPS), camera, microphone, photos, or contacts.
  • We do not operate a backend that receives your gameplay or personal data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays banner and full-screen (interstitial) advertisements through Google AdMob, a Google service, to support development. To serve, cap the frequency of, measure, and — where permitted — personalize these ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information from your device, including:

  • Your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android);
  • Your IP address and a coarse (approximate) location derived from it;
  • Technical device information such as device model, operating system version, language, and similar settings;
  • Ad interaction and app-activity data, such as ads shown, taps, and general usage related to advertising.

This information is collected and used by Google as an independent party under its own policies. We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you; we only see aggregate, anonymous reporting (for example, total impressions and estimated earnings). For details, see Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and the Google advertising notice.

4. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, before any cross-app tracking identifier (the IDFA) is used for personalized advertising, the App presents Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can allow or deny tracking, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you deny tracking, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant (non-personalized).

5. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where required by the GDPR and related laws, the App uses Google’s consent mechanism (the UMP SDK) to ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and to let you manage your choices. You can grant or withhold consent, and personalized advertising will only occur where you have consented.

6. Your US state privacy rights (including California)

We do not sell your personal information for money. However, the use of your advertising identifier for personalized advertising may be considered “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising, or a “sale,” under some US state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act. You can opt out of this at any time by declining the App Tracking Transparency prompt (iOS), withholding advertising consent, or limiting ad personalization in your device settings (see below).

7. Children

The App is a general-audience relaxation game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and ads served in the App are configured for a general audience, not for child-directed treatment. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we will address it.

8. Your choices and controls

  • Reset your locally-stored records from the Records or Settings screen.
  • iOS: allow or deny tracking via the App Tracking Transparency prompt, and limit ad personalization at Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • Android: reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization at Settings → Google → Ads.
  • EEA/UK/Switzerland: manage advertising consent through the in-app consent prompt.

9. Data retention and international processing

On-device data remains until you reset it or uninstall the App. Information collected by Google for advertising is retained and processed by Google under its own policies, and may be processed in the United States or other countries.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy: leandroviolim@gmail.com.
