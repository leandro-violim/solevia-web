Privacy Policy — Cap Kickers

Last updated: August 26, 2026

This page is maintained by the app owner (“we”, “us”) to explain how Cap Kickers (“the App”) handles information. The App is designed to be enjoyed without creating an account and without collecting personal information on our own servers.

Information stored on your device

The App saves your game settings, your chosen cap and pitch, and your campaign progress in local device storage so you can pick up where you left off. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the App removes it.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not require sign-in.
  • We do not collect your name, email, or contact information.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Advertising

The App displays advertisements to support development. When advertising is enabled in the store builds via Google AdMob, the ads SDK may collect information such as your device advertising identifier, coarse location derived from IP, app interaction and advertising data, and technical device information in order to serve and measure ads. On iOS you will be asked for App Tracking Transparency permission before any tracking identifier is used; declining still lets you play, you may simply see less-relevant ads. See Google’s advertising privacy notice at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Children

The App is a general-audience sports game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can change your ad tracking choice at any time (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking). You can limit ad personalization from your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising; Android: Settings → Google → Ads). Deleting the App removes the settings and progress stored on your device.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@solevia.app
