Privacy Policy — Cuckoo Club

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sole Via Entertainment LLC ("Solevia", "we", "us") handles information in connection with Cuckoo Club ("the App"), a one-phone party word game. You can play without an account: your Stars balance, owned cosmetics, seen-word history and Daily Round streak are saved on your device only. We collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used. The App is supported by ads through Google AdMob: our advertising partner (Google) collects certain device and usage information to show and measure ads, as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The App stores your Stars balance, owned card backs and avatars, which words you have already seen in each pack, the player names you type for a game, your settings, and your Daily Round streak in your device's local storage. This stays on your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the App erases it.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not require an account or sign-in.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or contact details.
  • We do not access your location (GPS), camera, microphone, photos, or contacts.
  • We do not receive information that personally identifies you; the only usage data we collect is anonymous and aggregated (see section 3).

3. Advertising and analytics (Google)

Analytics. The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics — such as when a round starts and ends, which pack is played, and which screens are used — so we can understand how the App is used and improve it.

Advertising. The App displays banner and rewarded-video ads through Google AdMob. Rewarded ads (a 24-hour themed pack, an extra hint for a caught imposter) are always optional; every core pack and mode is free without them. No ad is ever shown while a player is looking at their secret card or voting. To serve, cap, measure, and — where permitted — personalize ads, Google and its partners may collect and process: your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android); your IP address and a coarse location derived from it; technical device information; and ad interaction data. See Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and advertising notice (policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

4. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, before any cross-app tracking identifier (the IDFA) is used for personalized advertising, the App shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can allow or deny tracking and change your choice any time at Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

5. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where required, the App uses Google's consent mechanism (the UMP SDK) to ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

6. Your US state privacy rights

We do not sell your personal information for money. Using your advertising identifier for personalized ads may be considered "sharing"/a "sale" under some US state laws. You can opt out by declining the tracking prompt (iOS), withholding advertising consent, or limiting ad personalization in your device settings.

7. Children

The App is a general-audience family game. It does not ask for or collect names, ages or contact details; player names you type for a game stay on your device. "Kids mode" is a content setting that picks simpler words and pictures; it does not change data handling. The App is not part of Apple's Kids category or Google Play's Families program.

8. Data retention and international processing

On-device data remains until you reset it or uninstall the App. Information collected by Google for advertising is retained and processed by Google under its own policies.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@solevia.app
