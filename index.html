Casual · Relaxation

Zen Bubbles

Pop & Relax

The oddly satisfying pop of real bubble wrap, on a calm island journey. Clear a phase, watch the little bubble hop to the next one, and try to beat your best time.

  • Four island worlds — bubbles go off-grid, start drifting, then hide behind sliding shields.
  • Pop Challenge — a surprise mission with a surprise reward drops in every few hours.
  • Gear up — bombs, snowflakes and collectible bubble skins from the Shop.
  • Soft pops & haptics — calm music, and a light tap on every pop.
  • Free
  • Plays offline
  • Rated 4+ / Everyone
  • EN · PT · ES

Sports · Arcade

Cap Kickers

Bottle-cap finger soccer

The tabletop game half of Latin America grew up playing at school — futebol de tampinha — now in your pocket. Flick, don't aim: the harder the flick, the farther the cap flies.

  • Five touches a turn — thread a cap through the gap to keep possession, then shoot on the fifth.
  • Solo vs AI — beat each rival on the campaign road, from Rookie upward.
  • 2 Players & Practice — pass-and-play on one phone, or warm up with no pressure.
  • Trophy Cabinet — earn Caps by playing and unlock pitches and cap styles.
  • Free
  • Plays offline
  • Rated 4+ / Everyone
  • EN · PT · ES

Known as Futebol de Tampinha in Brazil and Fútbol de Tapitas across Spanish-speaking Latin America.

The studio

A small studio with a simple rule: respect the player’s time.

Sole Via Entertainment LLC is an independent studio based in Bay Harbor Islands, Miami. We design, build and publish our own games — from the first sketch to the store listing — and we keep them small on purpose: easy to learn, quick to play, satisfying to come back to.

Our games are free and supported by ads. That is a craft of its own, and we take it seriously: ads sit around the play, never on top of it, and nothing you need to enjoy the game is locked behind them.

No accounts
No sign-up, no email. Your records and settings stay on your device.
Plays offline
On a plane, in a queue, underground — the games work without a connection.
Three languages
English, Portuguese and Spanish, in the game and on the store.
Privacy by default
Anonymous usage stats you can switch off in Settings, and Apple’s tracking prompt honored.
