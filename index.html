Who we are

A small studio with a simple rule: respect people’s time.

Sole Via Entertainment LLC is an independent studio based in Miami, Florida. We make calm games and useful everyday apps for families, in English, Portuguese and Spanish. We design, build and publish them ourselves, from the first sketch to the store listing, and keep them small on purpose: easy to learn, quick to use, nice to come back to.

Our games are free to play and supported by ads. That is a craft of its own, and we take it seriously: ads sit around the play, never on top of it, and nothing you need to enjoy a game is locked behind them.

No accounts
No sign-up, no email. Your records and settings stay on your device.
Plays offline
On a plane, in a queue, underground — the games work without a connection.
Three languages
English, Portuguese and Spanish, in the game and on the store.
Privacy by default
Anonymous usage stats you can switch off in Settings, and Apple’s tracking prompt honored.
